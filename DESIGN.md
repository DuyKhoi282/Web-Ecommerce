## Brand & Style

This design system modernizes the omni-channel retail tech experience into an engineered, high-clarity commerce platform. Moving away from the chaotic visual noise typical of legacy consumer tech marketplaces, the aesthetic is structured around a crisp, high-contrast, modern architectural language that prioritizes product discovery, promotional velocity, and pricing transparency.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Urgent yet Composed:** High-octane sales events and flash deals communicate commercial dynamism without feeling spammy or cheap.
- **Precision Tech Authority:** Clean lines, deliberate spacing, and authoritative typography establish instant trust for big-ticket tech purchases (laptops, flagship phones, luxury electronics).
- **Frictionless Utility:** Engineered information architecture lets users scan technical specs, compare configurations, and assess warranty perks in milliseconds.

### Design Movement: Modern Clean E-Commerce
The visual language fuses minimalist structural clarity with punchy commercial accents:
- **Surface Tone:** Pure `#FFFFFF` and cool crisp `#F8FAFC` slate canvas eliminate dirty grey tones, making tech hardware cutouts pop naturally.
- **Chromatic Pacing:** Vibrant crimson (`#D70018`) is deployed deliberately for core conversion triggers, urgent countdown deals, and key interactive focal points, balanced by deep neutral slates and cool tech blue secondary accents.
- **Deliberate Compactness:** High visual density without optical clutter—engineered specifically for multi-SKU desktop browsing grids.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high optical contrast and disciplined functional attribution.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#D70018`):** The signature brand crimson. Reserved strictly for primary action buttons (Buy Now, Add to Cart), key promo headlines, live flash deal timers, and active filter tabs.
- **Secondary (`#2F80ED`):** An electric tech blue allocated to auxiliary utilities, installment calculators (Trả góp 0%), trade-in indicators (Thu cũ đổi mới), and clickable spec tags.
- **Tertiary (`#FF9900`):** Energetic amber dedicated to member loyalty (Smember points), review ratings, and limited bundle warnings.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate midnight providing near-black contrast for headings, sharp typography, and structural borders, replacing muddy low-contrast grays.

### Functional Shading & Surfaces
- **Deal Gradient (`#E11B22` to `#990011`):** Applied exclusively to high-urgency hero deal containers, flash sale marathon rails, and promotional header strips.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` on `#F8FAFC`):** Crisp pure-white card containers floating subtly above a soft slate canvas, eliminating the visual fatigue of flat single-plane layouts.

## Typography

The type scale combines the modern geometric energy of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent section headlines and currency figures with the bulletproof utilitarian legibility of **Inter** for dense spec sheets, product titles, and multi-line promotional clauses.

### Currency & Price Styling
- Active promotional prices leverage `price-hero` with an aggressive 800 weight in brand crimson (`#D70018`), accompanied by the Vietnamese currency symbol (`đ`) rendered at baseline.
- Historical anchor prices must utilize `price-strike` with `text-decoration: line-through` in `#94A3B8` (slate neutral) to maximize contrast against the active sale price.

### Title Truncation Rules
Product titles in standard cards use `title-md` clamped strictly to a maximum of 2 lines (`line-clamp: 2`) with an explicit fixed line-box height of 40px to ensure card grid alignment across all rows regardless of string length.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined desktop-first layout framework structured around a high-density, multi-tier e-commerce grid.

### Grid Framework & Max Width
- **Max Container Width:** 1200px fixed central viewport on desktop displays, preventing product cards from stretching into uncomfortable horizontal proportions on ultrawide monitors.
- **Desktop Columns (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with 20px (`gutter-desktop`) inter-card gap and 40px (`margin-desktop`) canvas padding.
- **Product Card Grids:** 5-column layout for standard category showcases (`repeat(5, minmax(0, 1fr))`), and 4-column layout for high-spec cards (laptops, monitors) requiring longer spec badges.
- **Mobile Grid (< 768px):** 2-column uniform product feed with 8px gutters and 12px outer canvas margins.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Compact internal component padding (`space-xs` to `space-md`) ensures high information density per viewport fold.
- Section vertical spacing is governed by `space-xl` (32px), creating distinct visual breaks between deal tracks, category carousels, and banner clusters without excessive negative space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach: micro-borders combined with ambient diffused shadows, abandoning harsh black drop shadows for clean, modern depth.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Surface):** Neutral canvas `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Resting Product Card):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a subtle 1px border in `#E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Card Hover & Interactive Cards):** On cursor hover, cards lift smoothly (`transform: translateY(-3px)`) accompanied by `0 10px 20px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` and border transition to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navigation / Category Bar):** `#FFFFFF` with 95% opacity backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) anchored by `0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` and a bottom border in `#F1F5F9`.
- **Level 4 (Popovers & Filter Flyouts):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 5 (Modals & Lightboxes):** Fixed central overlay with `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)` centered above an alpha backdrop `#0F172A` at 60% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a curated Level 2 (Rounded) shape language, delivering an inviting, contemporary tech aesthetic that balances hardware precision with consumer friendliness.

### Radius Assignments
- **Product Cards:** `12px` (0.75rem) corner radius with `overflow: hidden` to clip image containers cleanly.
- **Buttons & Search Inputs:** `8px` (0.5rem) corner radius for strong structural integrity and clickability.
- **Promo Badges & Deal Tags:** `4px` to `6px` for compact, tightly-spaced visual stamps.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Full-pill `9999px` to distinguish auxiliary filtering controls from primary product cards.

## Components

### 1. Product Cards (Core E-Commerce Atom)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 12px rounded radius, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 12px internal padding.
- **Image Showcase:** Square 1:1 aspect ratio container on `#FFFFFF` with seamless centered hardware cutouts. Smooth 1.05x image scale on parent hover.
- **Badge Slot (Top-Left):** Discount badge (`-15%`, `Trả góp 0%`) pinned with absolute positioning. Red badges use `#D70018` background with `#FFFFFF` bold text.
- **Title:** 2-line clamped `title-md`, `#0F172A`.
- **Pricing Block:**
  - Active Price: `price-hero` in `#D70018`.
  - Strikethrough Anchor: `price-strike` in `#94A3B8`.
- **Promo & Perk Ribbon:** Compact light slate or soft red box (`#FEF2F2`) with 1-line condensed perk (e.g., `Smember giảm thêm 1%`, `Tặng túi chống sốc`).
- **Footer Metadata:** Compact 5-star rating icon in `#F59E0B` alongside wishlisting heart icon.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Mua Ngay"):** Solid `#D70018` fill, `#FFFFFF` bold text, 8px radius, height 44px (desktop). Hover state: `#B30014` with subtle scale-up.
- **Secondary Action ("Trả Góp 0%"):** Solid `#2F80ED` fill or transparent border with `#2F80ED` outline and text.
- **Ghost / Neutral CTA:** 1px border `#E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text, hover fills with `#F1F5F9`.

### 3. Flash Sale & Countdown Deal Modules
- **Container Header:** Red-to-dark-crimson linear gradient (`deal-gradient-start` to `deal-gradient-end`), pure white typographic branding, alongside inline countdown pill boxes.
- **Countdown Timer Digit:** `#0F172A` text inside a rounded-md `#FFFFFF` pill container with high optical weight (bold 700).
- **Progress / Stock Bar:** Track background in `#FEE2E2`, filled bar in vibrant `#D70018` with subtle animated stripe or glow indicating remaining quantity.

### 4. Chips & Category Pills
- **Filter Chips:** 32px height, pill shape (rounded-full), 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active state switches to `#FEF2F2` background, 1px `#D70018` border, and `#D70018` font color.
- **Brand Quick-Selects:** White pill containers housing monochrome or full-color mini brand logos (Apple, Samsung, ASUS, Xiaomi).

### 5. Input Fields & Universal Search
- **Header Search Bar:** Height 42px, radius 8px, `#F1F5F9` background, zero border in resting state, `#0F172A` text.
- **Focused State:** Switches to `#FFFFFF` surface with 2px solid `#D70018` ring and elevation Level 2. Pre-pended search icon in slate gray and right-aligned quick-action clear button.

### 6. Badges & Promo Tags
- **Flash Tag:** `#D70018` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-xs`, uppercase tracking.
- **Smember Privilege Badge:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#B45309` text, subtle gold highlight.
- **Trade-in / Eco Badge:** `#EFF6FF` background, `#1D4ED8` text for device exchange incentives.
